Field Journal

Article Takeaways & Essays

FEATURE — NOV 2023 Ethno-microbiology & Synthetic Biology

Microbial Culture in Culture: Tapëy Rice Wine

Cultural Binding in Sagada

Field observation in Sagada highlighted tapëy as a social binder in Cordilleran heritage — accompanying sacred ‘pitík’ prayers and communal discussions at the dap-ay, using búbod starter cultures of molds, yeasts, and lactobacilli.

Standardizing Microbial Quality

Studies on starter cultures like Pichia kudriavzevii and S. cerevisiae show how food microbiology can standardize indigenous Philippine products, laying groundwork for Protected Designation of Origin (PDO) status.

Synthetic Yeast Horizons

Breakthroughs from the Sc2.0 synthetic yeast consortium in replacing 16-chromosome eukaryotic blueprints open doors for near-perfect búbod fermentation recipes that elevate local food microbiology globally.

ONCO-IMMUNOLOGY

Bacterial Extracellular Vesicles (BEVs) in Cancer

BEVs carry cytoplasmic cargo (DNA, RNA, virulence factors) that can cross blood–brain barriers and modulate tumors. Recombinant BEVs generated via genetic engineering hold promise as personalized cancer vaccines and non-invasive microbiome therapies.

BIOELECTRONICS

VIBES Pill & Mechanoreceptor Stimulation

The Vibrating Ingestible BioElectronic Stimulator (VIBES) pill activates gastric mechanoreceptors to induce serotonin release and metabolic responses mimicking full satiety — a new avenue for metabolic disease control.

IMMUNOTHERAPY

PD-1 Blockade (Dostarlimab) in Rectal Cancer

Evaluating 100% complete clinical remission rates in mismatch repair-deficient rectal cancer using Dostarlimab — highlighting the role of gut-microbiome immunomodulation in potentiating small biologics.

BACTERIAL MAGNITUDE

Candidatus Thiomargarita magnifica

Discovery of a centimeter-long megabacterium with compartmentalized DNA and membrane-bound organelles, challenging established limits on bacterial biovolume (by roughly 4,200 times) and cellular complexity.
